Pin positions are approximate — resort base area where known, town centre otherwise. Good enough to find a park, not survey-grade. Colour: orange = lift-served, green = opened 2022–26, teal = on Loam Pass.

Vertical drop is the one number every one of these parks reports, so it's the one that can be compared honestly across all of them. Trail mileage and lift counts are recorded inconsistently, so they're left on the individual cards rather than ranked here.

Vertical drop is hard to picture as a number. Stack a few parks side by side and the spread becomes obvious — the East Coast parks are not small, they're just next to Jackson Hole. Add as many as you like; peaks run west to east, and clicking one removes it.
